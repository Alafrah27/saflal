# About Saflal

Good conversations. Closer connections.

Saflal is a free chat app for adults who want to stay in touch through messages and familiar voices. Find people by name or nickname, send a friend request, and start a one-to-one conversation once it is accepted.

Send text messages, record a voice note, or make a one-to-one audio call on a supported Android device. Read indicators, unread counts and call history help you follow your conversations. Choose English or Arabic, including a right-to-left Arabic interface.

Manage your nickname and biography, choose notification preferences, and block or unblock users through the app. Settings also includes an account-deletion request. Our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) explains profile visibility, service providers and what deletion does and does not remove.

Saflal is independently operated from Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
