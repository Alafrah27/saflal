## 7. Deleting messages, history and accounts

The app distinguishes between these actions:

- **Delete your sent message for everyone:** removes its text or voice attachment from the conversation and leaves a deleted-message marker and related metadata. It cannot recall screenshots, earlier notifications, downloads or other copies outside Saflal.
- **Delete a conversation for yourself:** clears that history from your view. It does not delete the other participant's history or all underlying records. A later message can bring the conversation back into your list.
- **Delete call history:** hides selected calls from your own history, without erasing the shared call records.
- **Delete your account:** Settings provides a confirmed deletion request. End any active call first. Once accepted, the request disables your profile, removes specified profile details, and starts cleanup of associated account relationships and provider profiles. Completion can require retries if a provider is unavailable.

Account deletion currently preserves shared conversations, messages, referenced voice attachments and call records, together with identifiers and limited account/deletion metadata. Your profile is displayed as "Deleted user." Account deletion is not a promise that every record associated with you is erased or irreversibly anonymized. See the Privacy Policy for the exact limits and ways to request further deletion where applicable.

There is no current user-facing disappearing-message setting. Ordinary conversations do not automatically delete all messages after seven or thirty days. Do not rely on automatic erasure.
